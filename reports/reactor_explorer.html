Zero-power days by year

Forecast results are from the 2023–2025 test period, not a live forecast. Use the checkboxes above the chart to hide or show each model. A zero-power day is a day the NRC daily report lists the reactor at 0%, so days at reduced power are not counted. Hovering over or selecting a quarter also selects its year in the zero-power chart. Outages that cross New Year are split between the two years. Outage causes come from the NRC report text and can be wrong: Browns Ferry 1 was shut down from 1985 until 2007, which shows here as refueling. Hatched years have no data. Watts Bar 2 starts in October 2016, and Vogtle 3 and 4 are left out because they started too recently.

Source: Nuclear Reliability project, using NRC power reactor status reports.
